THE BELL OF SKÄRVIK

Act one: The silent bell

Scene 1: The harbour
Location: Skärvik harbour, early morning
Present: Harbour master Lovisa Berg

READ ALOUD:
Fog lies thick over the water. Somewhere out in the bay a ship's bell rings, slow and steady, though no ship has been seen for three days.

The fishing boats have stayed in port since Sunday. Lovisa Berg counts the days on a slate by the door and refuses to send anyone out until the bell stops.

SECRET: The bell belongs to the Hoppet, which sank with all hands forty years ago.

PERSON: Lovisa Berg
Mood: Tired and stubborn
Description: Harbour master for twenty years. Lost a brother at sea.
Linked to: The harbour, the Hoppet
She knows (public): No boat has gone out since Sunday.
She knows (secret): She heard her brother's voice in the fog last night.

If the characters row out into the fog, a Vigilance check lets them hear oars that are not their own.

Scene 2: The chapel
Location: The fishermen's chapel on the hill
Present: Sexton Nils Ek

HINT: The chapel's records list the crew of the Hoppet.

The sexton keeps the records in a chest behind the altar. He will only open it for someone who lights a candle for the drowned.
