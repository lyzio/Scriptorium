Scriptorum for Windows
======================

What you need
  64-bit Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11. Nothing else:
  Python, Qt and Typst are inside, and Scriptorum needs no internet
  connection.

Install
  Run Scriptorum-<version>-windows-x64-setup.exe. It installs for you alone
  and needs no administrator rights; choose "Install for all users" on the
  first page if everyone on the computer should have it.

"Windows protected your PC"
  Scriptorum is free software and is not signed with a paid certificate, so
  Windows SmartScreen asks you to confirm it. This is expected.
    1. Click "More info".
    2. Click "Run anyway".
  Your browser may also ask whether to keep the download: choose Keep.

After installing
  Scriptorum is in the Start menu. .scav adventures open in Scriptorum and
  .scmod modules offer to install when you double-click them, each with its
  own icon.

Update
  Run the newer setup program. Your adventures, modules and settings are
  kept (%APPDATA%\Scriptorum).

Remove
  Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Scriptorum > Uninstall. Your settings
  and modules stay in %APPDATA%\Scriptorum until you delete that folder.

Licence

  licences of every part inside are in Help > About Scriptorum. The source
