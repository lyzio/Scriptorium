Scriptorum for macOS
====================

What you need
  macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer. Use the download for your Mac:
    ...-macos-arm64.dmg    Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later)
    ...-macos-x86_64.dmg   Macs with an Intel processor
  Nothing else: Python, Qt and Typst are inside the app, and Scriptorum needs
  no internet connection.

Install
  1. Open the .dmg.
  2. Drag Scriptorum onto the Applications folder.
  3. Eject the .dmg.

The first start: "Apple could not verify Scriptorum..."
  Scriptorum is free software and is not signed with a paid Apple
  certificate, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. This is expected.

  macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer:
    1. Double-click Scriptorum. A message says it cannot be verified.
       Click Done (not Move to Bin).
    2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security.
    3. Next to "Scriptorum was blocked...", click Open Anyway, and confirm
       with your password.
  macOS 12 to 14:
    Right-click (or Control-click) Scriptorum in Applications, choose Open,
    and click Open in the message.

  After that, Scriptorum starts normally. .scav adventures and .scmod modules
  open in Scriptorum when you double-click them.

Update
  Drag the newer Scriptorum onto Applications and choose Replace. Your
  adventures, modules and settings are kept
  (~/Library/Application Support/Scriptorum).

Remove
  Drag Scriptorum from Applications to the Bin. Your settings and modules
  stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Scriptorum until you delete it.
