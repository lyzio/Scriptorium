An unofficial, fan-made look, not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of the work or game it is named after.
It uses no text, artwork or logos from any publisher. Its frames and
ornaments are the project's own drawings (tools/make_ornaments.py).

Fonts in fonts/ keep their own licences (SIL OFL 1.1 or Apache 2.0), each in
the licence file beside the font. The relationship chart uses fletcher 0.5.8
(MIT), CeTZ 0.3.4 (LGPL-3.0-or-later) and oxifmt 0.2.1 (MIT-0) in vendor/,
each with its licence file.
